Logistics & Supply Chain WhatsApp AI Dispatch Agent

Fleet Dispatch Automation for Multi-State Freight Carrier

Client Profile Regional freight company managing over 180 commercial trucks across 4 southeastern states.
Operational Problem Dispatchers were drowning under 400+ daily WhatsApp messages, load status inquiries, and arrival check-ins. Crucial arrival delays were lost in personal chat threads, causing dock hold charges.
Concise.ai Solution Architected an automated WhatsApp Dispatch Agent connected directly to their fleet management software and GPS telemetry API. The agent authenticates drivers by phone number, extracts load numbers, and logs arrival timestamps.
Qualitative Result Over 90% of routine status inquiries are resolved without human dispatcher input. Dispatchers shifted from reactive texting to strategic load planning, reducing weekend overtime burnout.

Instant driver status logging 24 hours a day.
Zero
Lost status messages or missed dock hold notifications.
Healthcare & Clinical Services 24/7 AI Voice Receptionist

24/7 Voice Receptionist for Multi-Location Dental Practice

Client Profile Rapidly growing group of 4 clinical dental practices with over 8,000 active patient files.
Operational Problem Front-desk receptionists were overwhelmed handling ringing phones while checking in patients. More than 35% of prospective patient calls arrived after 5:00 PM or during lunch hours, going to voicemail with a 70% non-callback rate.
Concise.ai Solution Deployed an ultra-natural conversational AI Voice Receptionist integrated with their Electronic Health Record (EHR) calendar system and patient database.
Qualitative Result All inbound phone calls are answered on the very first ring, after-hours bookings are scheduled directly to doctor calendars without double-booking, and front-desk staff can focus entirely on patient hospitality.

Seamless appointment booking across nights, weekends, and holidays.
